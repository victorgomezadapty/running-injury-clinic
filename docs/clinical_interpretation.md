# Clinical interpretation boundaries

The primary question concerns 3D running patterns associated with current injury status. Injury-positive severity and a clinician-recorded specific diagnosis are distinct variables. An unspecified pain entry is not promoted to a diagnostic category. Patients may have concurrent injuries and changing diagnoses across sessions.

Interpret a group difference only after speed and runner characteristics are examined. Statistical significance, clinical importance and diagnostic utility require separate evidence. Avoid statements that a movement caused an injury. The intended wording is that a biomechanical pattern was associated with an injury group.

An eventual Runner Biomechanical Profile can display supported hip, knee and ankle features, speed and comparison with a dataset reference distribution. Percentiles are not validated thresholds or rehabilitation targets. Group means do not describe every individual. Injured-side/contralateral representations require verified side encoding; bilateral, unknown and contradictory records need explicit handling. A contralateral limb may also be affected.

Running Biomechanics Intelligence is a future physiotherapist support concept. Milestone 1 does not provide treatment recommendations, diagnostic claims or future-risk estimates. Review the cohort, missingness and metadata quality before interpreting movement.
