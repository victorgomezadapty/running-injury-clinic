# Future integration

This project contributes movement and 3D kinematics to a broader Human Performance & Rehabilitation Intelligence architecture. ACL sEMG contributes neuromuscular function; a future Competitive Runners project contributes longitudinal training exposure; European Football Injuries contributes injury burden and availability. These are conceptual/architectural contributions, not permission to join unrelated people at row level.

Use namespaced source IDs, participant IDs, session keys, measurement units, protocol identifiers, provenance hashes and explicit feature definitions. Population reference summaries can be compared only after documenting compatible constructs and protocol differences. Never fill a Calgary participant's missing history from a different participant in another study.

The BMC–RIC interlaboratory supplement is prepared in parallel at Milestone 1 to make compatibility constraints explicit before later analysis. Keep separate source tables; treat any RIC overlap as unresolved until an author-provided participant crosswalk is verified. Later waveform harmonization, external validation and clustering remain behind the Milestone 1 review gate.
